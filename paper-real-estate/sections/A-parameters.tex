\section{Parameters and Data}
\label{app:parameters}

This appendix lists every object the model and the estimation use and the data each one comes from. Table~\ref{tab:app_data} describes the data sources. Tables~\ref{tab:app_seller} to~\ref{tab:app_policy} list the parameters by block: the seller side, the buyer side, the estimates from the reform, and the hiding-cost and renovation objects of Section~\ref{sec:policy}. The status column says how each object enters: \emph{observed} (read from the data), \emph{estimated} (an estimate from the data), \emph{computed} (a function of other estimates, nothing new estimated), \emph{calibrated} (set to match a moment or an external figure), or \emph{imposed} (an assumption that is not tested). Subscript $m$ denotes a market; the estimation uses 110 markets in the hedonic panel, those passing the volume filter enter the estimates.

\subsection*{Energy Costs from the Registry}
\label{app:energy_costs}

A dwelling of quality $\theta$ in market $m$ carries a capitalised energy cost $\zeta_{\theta,m}=\kappa\,p^E a(r,T)\,\bar E_{\theta,m}/P_m$, in the units of the log price gaps (a first-order approximation), with $\zeta_{0,m}>\zeta_{1,m}>0$. The conversion factor $\kappa p^Ea(r,T)/P_m$ is common to both qualities, so any energy cost can equivalently be expressed in kWh$_{\rm ep}$/m$^2$/yr. A silent dwelling of quality $\theta$ is assumed to cost the same to heat as a rated one. Table~\ref{tab:app_calib} gives the inputs. The consumption averages pool disclosed and withheld matched listings, since restricting them to disclosed listings would condition on the seller's own hiding decision and reintroduce the selection the anchor exists to avoid. The ten-year horizon is shorter than full capitalisation would justify, and deliberately so: it matches the typical holding period, and the energy-efficiency-gap literature finds buyers behaving as though they discount durable energy attributes over such truncated horizons \citep{allcott_is_2012}. Assumption~\ref{assum:energy_anchor} and Equation~\eqref{eq:energy_anchor} in Appendix~\ref{app:rho_identification} give the construction in full.

\subsection*{Independence of Information and Quality}
\label{app:independence}

Independence of information status and quality, $K\perp\theta$, is most plausible for transactions driven by exogenous life events such as succession following a death, court-ordered liquidation or divorce settlement, in which legal proceedings impose binding timing constraints that preclude any quality-contingent decision to obtain a certified EPC. In such cases the seller's information status is determined by administrative capacity and procedural deadlines rather than by the energy performance of the dwelling. Independence among forced sales does not by itself deliver it in the whole population, because sellers may select into audits by quality. Appendix~\ref{app:robustness} reports estimates that relax the treatment of high-quality hiding.
