Reviewer's expectation before reading: b = rho omega1 beta1 and Delta = rho omega1 (zeta0 - zeta*), so prices identify rho, omega1, beta1 only through rho omega1 beta1; the anchor fixes beta1 and so identifies rho omega1 (in the DiD, delta(rho omega1)), not rho apart from omega1; Delta/b = e-tilde = psi e + (1-psi) e^def, so the raw ratio Delta/(b e*) has limit e-tilde/e*, equal to 1 only if psi = 1 or zeta^def equals the silent pool's cost; under limited attention c0 is the premium in Partial Pooling, so the robustness exercises should report the LA hiding cost and psi-hat_delta.

## Findings
F1. MAJOR (not mechanical; high). A-robustness L65-102, table L36: the robustness section is computed on the full-attention cost c0-hat^FA = beta1-hat e-hat*, which L117 itself says is benchmark-only, then draws conclusions about the paper's hiding cost ("this is the paper's most consequential specification choice"; "read the concealment cost as the smaller figure throughout"; "the relative ordering ... across apartments and houses is unaffected"). The headline cost is the LA predicted premium (Section 4 L457, L603; Introduction L151) with mean Delta-hat (8.5 apartments, 3.9 houses: apartments above houses); c0-hat^FA gives 2.41 vs 3.45, the reverse order. By the zero-residual property the mean LA cost equals mean Delta-hat whatever e-hat* is (simulated: mean predicted premium 6.1172 before and after a +0.067 shift in e-hat*), so the LA cost does not move with the sigma1 assignment; Delta-hat compares silent with E/F/G listings under both cuts, so it changes with the threshold only because the sample changes (45 -> 40, 97 -> 100). Fix: label every cost figure in this section as the full-attention benchmark and add LA c0-hat and psi-hat_delta columns.
F2. MAJOR (not mechanical; high). L109, L124-133, L226-236, L67, L300-302: statements identify rho_m itself ("Point identification of rho_m"; "Buyer sensitivity emerges from the anchor as a genuine point estimate"; "About half the buyers ... price energy performance"), which conflicts with Section 4 (the anchor identifies delta(rho omega1), and rho only under omega1 = 1; Section 4 L225, L523, L547); the caveat at L117 is not carried into the statements. Failing case: (rho, omega1) = (1, 0.5) and (0.5, 1) give the same b, Delta and rho-hat = 0.5 (sympy difference 0); in the first case every buyer is sensitive. Fix: add omega1 = 1 to the hypotheses or restate in terms of rho omega1.
F3. MAJOR (not mechanical; high). Cor ratio_specification_test L150-159 and opening paragraph L112-115: plim r-hat = 1 relies on an assumption the corollary does not state, and the "about 1 regardless" opening is contradicted by this appendix's own data. Lemma model_bounds(ii) is a counterexample (PP before and after the reform with c0 fixed gives Delta-hat -> 0, so r-hat -> 0); with pre-reform energy pricing delta(b e) differs from delta(b) e*; in the data 40 of 45 apartment markets (89%) have r-hat > 1 (Fig rho_saturation note). Fix: add No Pre-Reform Energy Pricing and psi = 1 to the corollary's hypotheses and qualify L112.
F4. MINOR (not mechanical; high). Prop anchored L231, Prop scale_nonid L133: c0 stated as point-identified without restriction, but the proof works at a PP equilibrium and Prop what_identified(iii) gives only bounds at the corners. Failing case: sigma0 = 0 with true c0 = 2 b theta-bar; the formula returns b theta-bar. Add "in Partial Pooling".
F5. MINOR (mechanical for L283; high). Notation clash: beta1,m means b_m at L168, L174, L196, L231, while at L121-128 beta1 is the structural parameter and b = rho beta1; L283 writes Delta_m = rho_m beta1,m e*_m. L174 (Delta = beta1,m e) and L283 (Delta = rho beta1,m e) cannot both hold when rho < 1. Use b_m, as Section 4 does.
F6. MINOR (not mechanical; high). Lemma model_bounds(i) L196: the bound e <= theta-bar needs sigma1 = 0, not stated, while table rows 2 and 4 use sigma1 > 0. Counterexample: pi 0.5, theta-bar 0.625, sigma0 = 0, sigma1 = 1 gives e = 0.769 > theta-bar. Add sigma1 = 0.
F7. MINOR (not mechanical; high). L318-320: "a market can only be sorted into [a corner] when buyer sensitivity has been clipped" contradicts Prop what_identified(ii): c0-hat^FA = beta1-hat e-hat*(sigma0-hat) and the band [beta1-hat e(1), beta1-hat theta-bar] do not involve rho-hat. Clipping rho-hat to 1 leaves beta1-hat and sigma0-hat unchanged, so the classification is PP exactly when sigma0-hat is in (0,1). Delete the clause (looks like a leftover from an earlier construction).
F8. MINOR (not mechanical; medium-high). F9. MINOR (not mechanical; high). L298: "The two estimates ... differ by the wedge lambda-hat_m" holds only on average; market by market the gap is (1 - psi-hat) beta1-hat (eta - e-hat*). Example: beta1-hat 2, e-hat* 0.5, eta 2.3, psi-hat 0.13, Delta-hat 10: gap 3.13 but lambda-hat = 9. Say "on average".
F10. MINOR (not mechanical; high). L298: "a group the registry identifies precisely" is contradicted by tab:robustness: sigma0-hat is 15.2% or 5.9% depending on the sigma1 assignment (a factor of 2.6, L77). Qualify.
