## Findings
1. MAJOR (not mechanical; high). A-benchmark:19-23: prop:premium is false as stated with hiding costs: "(K,theta) strictly prefers disclosure iff theta > e(sigma0*)" and "(ii) theta = 0 strictly prefers non-disclosure whenever e > 0" ignore c0; it also covers K = 0 sellers who cannot disclose. Failing case: pi = theta-bar = rho = 0.5, beta1 = 1, c0 = 0.3: Cost-Deterred Hiding with e* = 0.5 > 0, and the low type's net gain from silence is -0.05, so she strictly prefers disclosure, contradicting (ii) and prop:equilibrium(i). Fix: state it gross of hiding costs (c0 = c1 = 0) and for K = 1 (04:304 already says "with c0 = c1 = 0").
2. MAJOR (not mechanical; high). m.tex:444-521: the proof of prop:premium, and through it cor:hq_disclose, uses a pricing model the benchmark does not use. B:11 and A-attention:415 say the results rest on linear pricing; the proof is built on gamma0, gamma1, G, phi, Pi-tilde from the logit model. The Preliminary FOC gamma1/(gamma0 + gamma1 p) = 1 - G(v1 - p) holds only for single-segment demand; Lemma A.1 has two segments rho G(v1 - p) + (1 - rho) G(v0 - p). m:515 then uses "By Lemma A.1, p0* > p1*", but Lemma A.1 is about profit, not price; A-attention:415 itself says two-segment price monotonicity needs a cross-partial condition. m:506 gives "rho in [0,1] >= 0" as the reason dPi-tilde/dv1 > 0, which needs rho > 0. Fix: the linear-pricing one-liner U(1,theta,0) - U(1,theta,1) = rho beta1 (e - theta) - (c0 + theta c1), or cite Prop att_incentives at psi = 1.
3. MAJOR (not mechanical; high). m.tex:573: the proof calls Partial Pooling "the empirically dominant regime". A-robustness:316-320 says every market lands in Partial Pooling "by construction" and reports no regime heterogeneity as an empirical finding; under limited attention \attRawNPpApt = 0 of 45 apartment and \attRawNPpHouse = 11 of 97 house markets are in Partial Pooling; Fig att_belief puts 37 of 45 apartment markets outside what the benchmark can produce. Delete or restate.
4. MAJOR (not mechanical; high). A-benchmark:134, 144-146; m.tex:683: the cross-market predictions and the "expanding" band do not follow from the model. Simulated with c0 independent of rho, rho ~ U[0.2,1], pi = theta-bar = 0.5: with c0 ~ U[0,1], corr(rho, premium | PP) = +0.93, not zero (selection into the band, which scales with rho); with c0 ~ U[0,0.15], P(corner | rho above median) = 1.000 vs 0.868 below the median, contradicting "higher rho-hat less likely to sit in a corner"; the band shifts up rather than nests ([0.167, 0.25] at rho 0.5 vs [0.20, 0.30] at rho 0.6; at c0 = 0.18, raising rho from 0.5 to 0.6 moves the market from PP with sigma0* = 0.78 into CAS); "weakly expanding ... strictly increasing width" contradicts itself. Fix: condition the predictions on c0/(rho beta1) or drop them; describe the band as "shifts up and widens".
5. MAJOR (not mechanical; medium-high). A-benchmark:165 (same wording m:347): the welfare paragraph's "transfer from buyers" and "pure rent" are wrong in the benchmark: buyers price the silent pool at its Bayesian mean, so the expected transfer is zero; uninformed sellers earn rho beta1 e* < rho beta1 theta-bar, so contamination costs them. Failing case: pi = theta-bar = 0.5, rho beta1 = 0.5, c0 = 0.2: e* = 0.4, sigma0* = 0.5; buyers pay 0.2 for expected value 0.2; uninformed sellers get 0.2 vs 0.25 with no hiding; an uninformed theta = 1 seller gets 0.2 vs her quality value 0.5; the payoff is a "rent" only relative to p_L. Author to restate the counterfactual.

## Sign of rho, worked out independently (confirms P2 finding 1)
In the benchmark, d sigma0*/d rho = theta-bar (1-pi)/[c0 pi (1-theta-bar)] > 0 in Partial Pooling (sympy). Globally sigma0* rises from 0 to 1 as rho goes from 0.01 to 0.99 at pi = theta-bar = 0.5, c0 = 0.2, and the premium is weakly increasing. Fewer energy-sensitive buyers make hiding LESS attractive. The benchmark material is consistent on this (B:126, 128, 140-143; m:707); only lower attention to silence (psi) raises hiding, and psi is a different parameter from rho. The Section 2 and Conclusion prose flagged in P2 is wrong in the same direction.
