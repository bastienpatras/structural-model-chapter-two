## Findings
F1. MINOR (not mechanical; high). 04-structural:114-119 (Assn attention, psi definition) and A-attention:105, 131-133. lem:att_prices, prop:att_incentives, cor:att_hq, prop:att_equilibrium: omega1 > 0 (equivalently b > 0) is used throughout, stated only in prop:att_seller_types ("with b = rho omega1 beta1 > 0"), and Assumption attention allows omega1 = 0. Failing case: omega0 = omega1 = 0 (allowed by omega0 <= omega1): psi and e-tilde are 0/0; with c0 = c1 = 0 all three prices are equal and (sigma0, sigma1) = (0.5, 0.5) is an equilibrium, contradicting cor:att_hq ("sigma0* < 1 implies sigma1* = 0"). Fix (author's decision): state omega1 > 0 where psi is defined, or add "with b > 0" to each statement.
F2. MINOR (not mechanical; high). 04-structural:339 vs A-attention:162, 229. prop:att_equilibrium(ii) characterises only sigma1* in {0,1}. When g1-bar(0) < c0 + c1 < g1-bar(1) and psi > 0 an interior sigma1* also exists, giving three equilibria, not "at least two". The appendix relies on them (Step 5 "an interior crossing"; att_seller_types Step 10 "At an interior sigma1*, Delta* = b + c0 + c1") but the proposition never states them. Failing case: the proof's own example (pi 0.5, theta-bar 0.6, e^def 2.3, psi 0.5) with (c0 + c1)/b = 0.4: brute force gives (1,0), (1,1/3), (1,1); brentq sigma1* = 0.33333. Fix: add "sigma1* in (0,1) is an equilibrium iff g1-bar(sigma1*) = c0 + c1".
F3. MINOR (not mechanical; high). A-attention:253, cor:att_ordering Step 4: "With psi = 1, psi-bar(theta-bar) < 1 = psi" is false when e^def < theta-bar and undefined when e^def = theta-bar. psi-bar(e) = (e^def - 1)/(e^def - e). Failing case: e^def 0.2, theta-bar 0.6: psi-bar(theta-bar) = 2.0 > 1; the "cursed default" (zeta^def = prior mean cost, 04:112 and the footnote at 04:310) gives e^def = theta-bar, zero denominator. The conclusion 0 < Delta* < b survives through the second sentence and Step 3. Fix: drop the first sentence of Step 4 or restrict it to e^def > 1.
F4. MINOR (not mechanical; high). 04-structural:363, 365 (Table att_equilibria, column "Delta* > b iff"): "psi < psi-bar(theta-bar)" and "psi < psi-bar(e(1))" are offered as iff conditions without the guards zeta^def < zeta-bar^sil and e^def > 1 that prop:att_incentives(iii) and att_seller_types carry. Failing case: CDH with e^def 0.2, theta-bar 0.6, psi 0.5: the table's condition holds (psi-bar(theta-bar) = 2) but e-tilde = 0.4 < 1, so Delta* < b. Fix: "e^def > 1 and psi < psi-bar(.)".
F5. NIT (mechanical; high). A-attention:74, lem:att_prices Step 1: the lemma assumes Assn linear_pricing, the proof cites "the pricing rule used to derive eq:premium_derived" (m.tex:585), which points back to eq:mean_valuation (m.tex:583): a circular pointer to a full-attention derivation. Fix: "By Assumption~\ref{assum:linear_pricing}, a listing's price is...".
