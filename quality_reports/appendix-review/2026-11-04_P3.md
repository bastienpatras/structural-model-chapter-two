## Findings
1. MAJOR (not mechanical, high). A-attention:470 vs 04-structural:590: the appendix reads the bound violations as evidence of a second force ("40 percent of markets in the top tercile also exceed it ... probably not the only one"); the body says about half would exceed it from sampling error alone, "so the count does not point to a second force". 21/45 = 47% and 40% are at or below the 50% the body attributes to noise. Author to choose one reading, state it in both places.
2. MAJOR (not mechanical, high on algebra, medium on magnitude). A-attention:408: dependence on linear pricing is scoped too narrowly (only "all propositions of Section ss:att_equilibrium"). The inversion e-tilde = Delta/b, hence psi-hat_delta and Proposition att_identification, also need it. Exact three-segment logit (V-bar 12, zeta^ins 1, rho 0.5, omega1 1, zeta0 3, zeta1 1, e 0.6, zeta^def 0): at psi = 0.13 the exact ratio is 1.239 against the linear 1.383; plugging it into the linear inversion gives psi-hat 0.29, not 0.13. Extend the caveat to Sections 4.4-4.5.
3. MAJOR (not mechanical, high). A-attention:453 (Step 4), repeated at :480 and :461: "This biases both estimators toward full attention." Macros_mc: the regression through the origin gives 0.33-0.70 without selection and 0.30-0.60 with selection, so selection lowers it. Ratio of sums: selection pushes toward psi = 1 only if [(1-e)s_L^2 - e s_H^2]/[(eta-e)(s_H^2+s_L^2)] < 1 - psi; for houses (psi 0.95, e about 0.59, eta 2.11, s_L = 3, s_H = 0.3) Monte Carlo gives 0.951 without and 0.936 with selection (away from 1). For apartments the condition holds for any split. State the condition; limit the claim to the ratio of sums in apartments; report the regression-through-origin result as it is. The Figure F3b caption (:480) states the toward-one bias for both segments.
4. MINOR (not mechanical, medium). A-attention:463 (Step 6) and Table 6 notes 04:573: "the point estimate equals the upper end of the identified interval ... identities of the aggregation" holds only if zeta0,m - zeta1,m is constant across markets. 45 synthetic markets with heterogeneous gaps: psi-hat 0.298 vs zeta*_w/zeta-bar^sil_w 0.274; the default at which psi-hat reaches 0 is 73.8 kWh vs a weighted zeta* of 68.9. At the reported values 31/244 = 0.127, 233/245 = 0.951, within rounding. Say what is an identity (zero residual sum, Prop (iii) market by market) and what holds approximately.
5. MINOR (not mechanical, medium).
