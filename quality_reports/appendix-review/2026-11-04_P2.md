## Reviewer's own reading (before the proofs)
In Partial Pooling the closed form is e* = (c0/b - (1-psi) e^def)/psi and sigma0* falls as e* rises: sigma0* decreasing in psi when e^def > e*, decreasing in c0, increasing in rho omega1 (through b), weakly decreasing in zeta^def, decreasing in pi given e*. The premium Delta* = c0 does not depend on rho in Partial Pooling; in the corners Delta* = b x constant and rises with rho; b = rho omega1 beta1 always rises with rho. Band = [Delta_psi(e(1)), Delta_psi(theta-bar)], width psi b (theta-bar - e(1)). The proofs agree with all of this.

## Findings
1. MAJOR (not mechanical; high on the model, medium on how the prose should be read). The prose on rho has the opposite sign to the model's comparative static. `02-unraveling-draft.tex` L86-87 ("a demand side in which many buyers do not price the attribute weakens the penalty that would otherwise discipline them") and `main.tex` L343 Conclusion ("buyer heterogeneity makes exploiting that ambiguity financially worthwhile") gloss prop:att_cs(i), prop:comparative_statics(ii) and cor:rho. In the model, lower rho lowers sigma0* in Partial Pooling, lowers Delta* in the corners, and leaves Delta* unchanged in Partial Pooling; rho < 1 is not needed for silence to sell above a poor rating. Failing case: pi 0.5, theta-bar 0.6, beta1 10, omega1 1, psi 0.5, e^def 2.3, c0 7; as rho goes 0.485, 0.49, 0.50, 0.51, sigma0* goes 0.057, 0.192, 0.500, 0.870, b goes 4.85 to 5.10, Delta* stays at 7.000. In the full-attention benchmark with c0 = 0, sigma0* = 1 and Delta > 0 at rho 0.3 and 0.9: pi < 1 alone puts silence above a poor rating. Fix: restate the rho channel as the revenue channel (more sensitive buyers raise b, Delta in the corners, and sigma0*); Section 2 L97-99 already uses this direction. NOTE FOR THE AUTHOR: the Conclusion sentence at L343 was rewritten in this session on 2026-11-03 ("The first two put silence above a poor rating: uninformed sellers keep it ambiguous, and buyer heterogeneity makes exploiting that ambiguity financially worthwhile"); that rewrite reproduced the claim and is wrong in the same way. The abstract now says only that buyers "differ in how sensitive they are to energy efficiency", which is safe.
