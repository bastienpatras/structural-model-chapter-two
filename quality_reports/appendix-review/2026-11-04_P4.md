## Findings
1. MAJOR (not mechanical; medium-high). A-attention 723-764 vs main.tex 316, 331, 335 and the bill at 234: the saving bound covers the average of seller-specific minimum subsidies S_i*, but the text reads it as "the saving to the state" and sets it against a bill that pays one S*_m per market. Failing case (apartment-like inputs pi .512, theta-bar .625, psi .13, eta 2.32, b 4.1, costs 4.5 + Exp(25), sigma0-hat 0.151): at R-hat = 3 the average fall in S_i* is 0.173, the bound 0.452; the market-uniform full-take-up subsidy max_i S_i* falls by 2.99, 6.6 times the bound; the budget comparison then fails by up to 1/sigma0-hat. Fix: say in the statement that transfers are seller-specific, or bound the uniform-subsidy saving separately.
2. MAJOR, proof wrong, conclusion survives (not mechanical; high). A-attention 651 and 661: Step 3 says the belief after silence "is the limit of the pool belief as pi -> 1"; along the PP path e* does not depend on pi, so the limit is e*, not 0 (numerical: at pi = .5, .9, .99, .999 the pool belief stays 0.5800 while sigma0* falls from 0.207 to 0.0002). The proof contradicts itself: Step 5 calls the premium "discontinuous at pi = 1", which happens only under belief 0. The intuitive-criterion reading does deliver 0: at apartment values the high type's best-case gain from silence is -3.88 (equilibrium-dominated), the low type's +0.22; the proof states but does not show it. Fix: drop the limit argument and prove the domination inequality b psi (1 - e*) < b.
3. MINOR (not mechanical; high). main.tex 324 (statement) vs A-attention 762 (proof): the statement says the saving is zero "when all sellers share one hiding cost", the proof shows it only "in Partial Pooling". Counterexample: single cost c0 = 5, b = 4, pi .5, theta-bar .6, psi .13, e^def 2.32: Delta_psi(e(1)) = 8.30 > c0, so CAS with h = 3.30 and R-bar = 0.70; after psi -> 1 the gain b theta-bar = 2.40 < c0, CDH, R-bar = 4; S* falls by 3.30, not 0. Add "in Partial Pooling".
4. MINOR (not mechanical; medium-high). main.tex 331: "If the subsidy required her to display ... the cap would be absent": "costs independent of quality" plus "only low-quality sellers hide" forces c_i >= g1 = g0 - b, so r_i <= b for every seller; the cap holds through the rent, display rule or not. In apartments g1 = 4.5 (\attExcessApt), so r_i <= 8.5 - 4.5 about b = 4.1; the display figures (0.085, 0.117 bn) are a looser bound on the same object, not a different saving. Reword or drop "only lows hide" for the display variant.
5. MINOR (not mechanical; high). main.tex 271, Prop instruments(ii): "only the price of silent listings falls, by lambda" holds at the fixed hiding rate; once hiding responds the fall is lambda - b(theta-bar - e*) in a market that moves to CDH (apartment-like: lambda 6.17, equilibrium fall 6.03). main.tex 301 says "at a fixed hiding rate"; the proposition does not.
6. MINOR (not mechanical; high on algebra, medium on dispersion). main.tex 194: "to within about one percent of the rating premium" reads as a bound but 0.4 and 0.9 are means (a house market with sigma0 .20, pi .6, theta-bar .6, psi .95 gives 6.1% of b; house-mean inputs already give 1.02%); "exceeds S* by psi b (theta-bar - e*)" holds only if R-hat >= b (at R-hat = 0.5 b the excess is 0, not 0.019 b). Say "averages" and write the excess as (R-hat - b + psi b (theta-bar - e*))^+ - S*.
7. MINOR (mechanical; high). Units: A-attention 784; main.tex 204 and 213: R-bar_m = b-hat_m P_m treats b-hat as a fraction while the section reports b-hat in log points and Prop att_saving writes 100 R-hat/P_m "in log points". b-hat 4.1 log points and P about 3000 give 12,300 EUR/m2 against a reported mean of 122. Proof Steps 5-6 (A-attention 753-755) use min{lambda, b, R-hat} with R-hat in EUR/m2 while the statement uses 100 R-hat/P_m. Write b-hat_m P_m/100.
8. MINOR (not mechanical; medium). main.tex 268: "In apartments this [e^def >= theta-bar] follows from Delta-hat > b-hat" is true only for markets with Delta-hat_m > b-hat_m (37 of 45); for the other 8, without the zero default, e-tilde_m < 1 is compatible with e^def < theta-bar (e^def 0.4, theta-bar 0.6, psi .13).
9. MINOR (not mechanical; high). main.tex 270 and A-attention 654-659: "removes hiding whenever c0 >= b (1 - psi) e^def" fails at equality: at psi = 0, PP has c0 = b e^def = b (1 - psi) e^def, the seller is indifferent and every sigma0 is an equilibrium (R-bar = b still holds; ledger I3). 10. MINOR (not mechanical; medium). A-attention 772 and main.tex 169: the mean ratio 0.66 (0.76) includes 9 (12) markets where "the threshold depends on which equilibrium is selected"; the selection is not stated, nor whether the mean is over CAS markets or all markets; R-bar is either b - h or 0 on those 9 markets, moving the CAS mean by up to 9/20.
11. MINOR (not mechanical; medium). main.tex 139-140 (welfare remark): the remark charges the subsidy at mu S and lists a buyer-to-seller transfer as a "social gain"; it is correct only if Delta-W counts the subsidy the seller receives, but Delta-W(R-hat) is written as independent of S.
