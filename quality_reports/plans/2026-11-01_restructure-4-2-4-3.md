## Assessment (what is wrong)

**Ordering.** Current dependency order is players -> buyers -> beliefs -> prices -> effective belief -> seller payoffs. The natural order puts the seller's choice second, written over three prices (p_H, p_L, p_sil) that the rest of the section then derives.
- Used before defined: theta-bar (line 151, defined 222), pool belief e (122, derived 257), psi / omega_d / zeta^def (105, defined 153-158), Gamma and G (205-208), e* (292, undefined in 4.2), R-bar and sigma_0* (362), several forward results (Prop. att_incentives(iii) at 151, fig:att_belief at 144).
- Defined long before use: eta (127, needed at 294), Independence (221, belongs next to pi), sigma_theta (227, a seller strategy defined in the beliefs part), p_H/p_L/p_sil (defined mid-belief paragraph, 269).

**Seams.** Worst: 312 -> 317 (feedback loop -> "In French residential markets..."), then 87 -> 94, 107 -> 118, the stacked definitions at 132-146 (Gross Value has no lead-in), 266 -> 269, 362 -> 367 (4.4 repeats the reduction made at 324).

**Soundness items to fix on the way.**
1. No equilibrium concept is named (state PBE; price carries no signal, beliefs condition on d only).
2. The timing rationale (loi ALUR, DPE appended at signing) does not imply d and p^list are chosen together; reword.
3. Line 105 overstates: price gaps depend on rho*omega_1 given psi and zeta^def; price levels depend on rho separately. "rho identified when omega_1 = 1" is a normalisation. Reword.
4. phi*Gamma payoff is introduced, then Gamma is normalised to one, which makes phi, gamma, Gamma and "paid whether or not a sale occurs" idle. Remove from the core.
5. c(theta, d) omits K (uninformed would pay c_0); write c(K, theta, d) or use U(K, theta, d) only.
6. Strict monotonicity of e needs thetabar in (0,1); state it.
7. "e-tilde > 1 iff psi < 1 and e^def > 1" is a necessary condition; the exact condition is (1-psi)(e^def - 1) > psi(1 - e).
8. The independence argument (forced sales) does not deliver K independent of theta in the population; soften and point to the robustness appendix.
9. "Price = mean valuation" mixes log prices and level valuations; make it a labelled Assumption (Linear Pricing) with the first-order-approximation remark.

**Notation.** q (detection probability vs quality scale q(zeta)), r (compliance cost vs discount rate), beta_n vs beta_1, beta-hat_1 estimates b not beta_1, Delta / Delta(e) / Delta_psi(e) need one sentence telling them apart, No Falsification is an unlabelled bold paragraph, duplicated definitions (Table att_buyers vs Definition buyer valuation; the gains stated at 324, in Table att_payoffs's note, and in 4.4).

## Transition logic (drafted wording, to be adapted in the author's voice)

Each part opens with the question the previous part left, not with "we now define". Drafts (LaTeX in the working notes; the author owns final wording because these are `[AUTHOR REWRITE]`-class passages):
- Part 1: "The wedge turns on what buyers can infer from silence, so the first question is who stays silent and what each side knows when the listing goes up."
- Part 2: "An informed seller knows her quality, so the next question is what silence costs her and what it buys; we take the prices p_H, p_L and p_sil as given until part 5."
- Part 3: "Those prices are set by what buyers are willing to pay, which depends on the energy cost each type subtracts from a dwelling's value."
- Part 4: "Only the attentive buyer forms a belief about a silent listing, and that belief depends on who sits in the silent pool."
- Part 5: "Prices aggregate the attentive buyers' belief with the default of the inattentive ones, so the remaining question is what the market behaves as if it believed about silence."
- Bridge: "With prices and payoffs fixed, the remaining question is where the hiding rates settle, since each informed seller's choice moves the pool belief that sets the other's gain."
Style rules for the full pass: prose over step announcements, at most one em-dash per paragraph, few colon-elaborations, no "not X but Y", no one-line closers that restate, no staged openers, no stock words.
