## Introduction

I1. L64-66 (closer). Current: "... formalised by \citet{jung_kwon_1988}. Here it is measured rather than inferred."
    Suggest: "... formalised by \citet{jung_kwon_1988}; we measure it rather than infer it."

I2. L105 (inflation). Current: "a vantage point that, to our knowledge, is unique in this literature."
    Suggest: "a vantage point we know of no other study in this literature to share."

I3. L129-131 (closing summary of the findings). Current: "Together these facts are inconsistent with unraveling as a description of this market, while pointing to the mechanism that defeats it."
    Suggest: "These facts are inconsistent with unraveling here and point to what defeats it:" and keep the next sentence.

I4. Policy paragraph (¶12.5), last sentence repeats ¶12 (L173-178), which already says the three instruments act on separate margins.
    Suggest cutting from "Enforcement against strategic hiding changes who hides, ..." to the end, or cutting the same claim from ¶12. Keep the "outside Complete Adverse Selection" qualifier in whichever stays.

I5. ¶12 L173 (stacked qualifier). Current: "In equilibrium it does not, except in the corner where every informed low-quality seller hides." and L178 repeats "The result holds outside the corner in which every informed low-quality seller hides, and with one concealment cost per market."
    Suggest: delete the L173 clause and keep the L178 sentence, which states the scope once.

## Conclusion

C1. L342 (closer). Current: "... the opposite of what standard unraveling theory predicts. This paper resolves the puzzle."
    Suggest: "... the opposite of what standard unraveling theory predicts. Three departures from the classical framework resolve it."

C2. L344 (redundancy). "each empirically motivated and individually insufficient" and the last sentence "None of the three suffices alone: ..." say the same thing.
    Suggest: cut "each empirically motivated and individually insufficient" from the opening sentence.

C3. L347 (staging). Current: "This indifference condition yields the paper's central theoretical result. The non-disclosure premium equals exactly the concealment cost in partial pooling, ..."
    Suggest: delete the first sentence and begin "The indifference condition gives the paper's central theoretical result: the non-disclosure premium equals the concealment cost in partial pooling, ..."

C4. L350 (staging). Current first sentence: "The policy implications depart from textbook prescriptions in one respect."
    Suggest: delete it; the next sentence starts with "Although the renovation threshold mechanically incorporates the concealment cost".

C5. L353-354 (not-X-but-Y and repetition of L344). Current: "The policy challenge is not to punish opacity more severely but to produce knowledge in the first place."
    Suggest: "Punishing opacity more severely does little where sellers lack the information to disclose; producing that information does more." Also cut "Involuntary non-disclosers shelter strategic hiders, and silence cannot be read as bad news." (stated at L344).

C6. Redundancy across sections (narrativize). The three findings are stated in full in the abstract, Introduction ¶9, the Section 4 closer, and the Conclusion L344-347.
    Suggest: the Conclusion keeps one sentence on the apartment/house attention contrast, the lower-bound policy claim, and the open items (heterogeneous hiding costs, certified consumption, the commune take-up test); the three-departures paragraph then shortens to the uninformed-sellers and buyer-heterogeneity points.
