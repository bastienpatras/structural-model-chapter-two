{{M004}} Independence is most plausible for transactions driven by exogenous life events (succession following a death, court-ordered liquidation, or divorce settlement), in which legal proceedings impose binding timing constraints that preclude any quality-contingent decision to obtain a certified EPC. In such cases, the seller's information status {{M001}} is determined by administrative capacity and procedural deadlines rather than the energy performance of the dwelling; a seller under a judicial mandate to liquidate an estate has neither the incentive nor the discretion to condition audit acquisition on {{M002}}. In France, forced transactions of this type constitute a substantial share of the resale market, providing a natural mechanism through which {{M003}} obtains in the data.
