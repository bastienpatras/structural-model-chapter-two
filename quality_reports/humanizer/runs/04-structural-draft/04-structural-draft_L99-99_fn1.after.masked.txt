The negotiation margin in France is empirically small. The majority of properties transact at or very near list price, and the average discount rarely exceeds three to five percent. {{M001}} document similar patterns for England, showing that most residential transactions involve little or no negotiation once a listing price is set. {{M002}} provide evidence that list prices act as anchors that constrain final transaction prices. For France specifically, {{M003}} documents that the average negotiation margin has remained narrow and declining, with a large share of properties selling without any price reduction.
