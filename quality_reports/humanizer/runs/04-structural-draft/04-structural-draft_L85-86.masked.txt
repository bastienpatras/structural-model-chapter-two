The key friction is that buyers observe {{M001}} but not the seller's information status {{M002}}: they cannot distinguish a seller who chose not to disclose from one who could not. A buyer then arrives and decides whether to purchase at {{M003}}. With probability {{M004}}, the buyer is {{M008}}EPC-sensitive{{M009}} and incorporates energy performance into her valuation; with probability {{M005}}, the buyer is {{M010}}EPC-insensitive{{M011}} and conditions her bid solely on observable attributes. Sellers cannot observe buyer type ex ante and optimize against expected demand.
