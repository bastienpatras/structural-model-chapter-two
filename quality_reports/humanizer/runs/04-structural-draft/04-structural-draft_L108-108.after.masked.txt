{{M001}} is an institutional cost of concealment, fixed within the prevailing enforcement regime, which we recover from the equilibrium price gap in Section{{M004}}{{M005}}. It is also a policy object, a margin the regulator can shift through stricter enforcement, and Section{{M006}}{{M007}} studies counterfactual variation in {{M002}} on that basis.
