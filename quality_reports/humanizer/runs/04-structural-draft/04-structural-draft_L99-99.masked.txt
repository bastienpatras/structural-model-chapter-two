In French residential markets, listing prices anchor negotiation rather than serving as posted take-it-or-leave-it offers, and the transaction price conditional on a sale typically falls below the listing price by a margin that depends on local market conditions and property characteristics.{{FN1}} Buyer arrival and match quality are stochastic, so the seller faces uncertainty over both whether a sale occurs and, conditional on a sale, at what price.
