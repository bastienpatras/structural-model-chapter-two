Table~\ref{tab:who_hides} summarizes disclosure behavior by seller type. Uninformed sellers have no choice, since they cannot disclose information they do not possess (No Falsification Assumption). Informed sellers choose strategically, weighing the revenue gain from disclosure against the option of pooling with the non-disclosing mass. We denote by $\sigma_\theta \equiv \Pr(d=0 \mid K=1, \theta)$ the equilibrium hiding probability for an informed seller with quality $\theta$.
