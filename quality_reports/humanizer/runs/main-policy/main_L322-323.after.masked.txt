In houses the same exercise moves {{M021}} of {{M022}} markets into Complete Adverse Selection at once, with a mean hiding rate of {{M023}} percent. With one hiding cost per market every informed seller faces the same comparison, so the rise in the rating premium by a factor of about {{M001}} pushes the gain from hiding past that cost for all of them together. With costs that differ across sellers only those whose cost lies below the new gain would start hiding. We regard the jump as a feature of the single cost and report the bound for apartments only.
