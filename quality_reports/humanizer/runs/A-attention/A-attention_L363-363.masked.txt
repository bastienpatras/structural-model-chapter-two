All propositions of Section{{M001}}{{M002}} are derived under the linear pricing approximation of{{M003}}{{M004}}. Under the exact demand{{M005}}{{M006}}, buyers form three segments and the seller's optimal listing price is not a function of the mean sensitive valuation alone, so the propositions do not carry over automatically. For the two-segment demand{{M007}}{{M008}}, the optimal listing price is nondecreasing in the sensitive buyers' valuation whenever the cross-partial condition of the monotone-comparative-statics argument holds. We conjecture, but have not shown, that the orderings of Propositions{{M009}}{{M010}}--{{M011}} survive exact pricing at the estimated logit scale. With attention {{M012}}shares{{M013}} rather than a representative weight, a seller may also price to the inattentive segment and accept a lower probability of sale, which predicts longer time on market for silent listings at higher prices.
