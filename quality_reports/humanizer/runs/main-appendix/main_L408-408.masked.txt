What sets this approach apart is our ability to match these listings with data from ADEME (the French Environment and Energy Management Agency), which maintains an official registry of certified energy audits. These audits are reported by third-party assessors who are presumed to be honest and impartial. We match listings to the ADEME registry based on key characteristics that are registered in both databases, such as addresses, floor, item type, and area{{FN1}}.
