One further rule took effect six months after the reform. From 1 January 2022, an advertisement for the sale of a dwelling must state the estimated annual energy expenditure for standard use, with the reference year of the energy prices used, and a dwelling rated F or G must carry the mention ``logement \`a consommation \'energ\'etique excessive''. A displayed rating therefore reaches the buyer together with a euro figure, while a listing that shows no rating carries neither. Section~\ref{ss:attention} builds on this asymmetry.
