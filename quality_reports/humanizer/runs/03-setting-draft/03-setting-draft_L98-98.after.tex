\noindent Strategic hiding is rare but economically meaningful: 3.5\% of apartment listings and 1.1\% of house listings come from informed sellers who chose not to disclose. Pooling across listings, the hiding rate among informed sellers ($K=1$) is 6.9\% for apartments and 2.7\% for houses; the corresponding unweighted means across markets, reported as $\hat\sigma_{0,m}$ in the results, are 7.7\% and 3.7\%. Silence is uncommon relative to non-verification: only 16.7\% of apartment listings and 8.6\% of house listings are silent sellers with no registry record, and these are the non-disclosers who provide involuntary cover for strategic hiders. The quantitatively dominant group is the 32.2\% of apartment listings and 51.2\% of house listings display a score with no matching registry record. Taken together, sellers without a post-2021 registry record account for 48.8\% of apartment listings and 61.1\% of house listings, but the large majority of them do disclose a score.
