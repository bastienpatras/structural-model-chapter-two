{{M003}} Strategic hiding is rare but economically meaningful: 3.5{{M004}} of apartment listings and 1.1{{M005}} of house listings come from informed sellers who chose not to disclose. Pooling across listings, the hiding rate among informed sellers ({{M001}}) is 6.9{{M006}} for apartments and 2.7{{M007}} for houses; the corresponding unweighted means across markets, reported as {{M002}} in the results, are 7.7{{M008}} and 3.7{{M009}}. Silence is uncommon relative to non-verification: only 16.7{{M010}} of apartment listings and 8.6{{M011}} of house listings are silent sellers with no registry record, and these are the non-disclosers who provide involuntary cover for strategic hiders. The quantitatively dominant group is the 32.2{{M012}} of apartment listings and 51.2{{M013}} of house listings display a score with no matching registry record. Taken together, sellers without a post-2021 registry record account for 48.8{{M014}} of apartment listings and 61.1{{M015}} of house listings, but the large majority of them do disclose a score.
