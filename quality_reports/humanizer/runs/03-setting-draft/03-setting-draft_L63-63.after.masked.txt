The full listing sample covers 2021Q3 to 2024Q4, the post-reform period during which EPC disclosure is legally binding and DPE scores are credible to buyers. The apartment sample comprises 843,621 listings and the house sample 687,878 listings. For structural estimation we restrict to 2022 to 2024: the H2{{M004}}2021 period (the six months immediately following the reform) is excluded because the market had not yet fully repriced the DPE (hedonic estimates of {{M001}} are near zero or negative in that window), yielding 767,000 apartment listings and 633,000 house listings for the structural sample. For 51.2{{M005}} of apartment listings and 40.3{{M006}} of house listings, the ADEME match yields {{M002}}; the remainder are classified as genuinely uninformed ({{M003}}, no verified audit on record). For identification of market-level price indices, we use the panel of hedonic price indices constructed in {{M007}}, covering 110 markets (attraction zones, {{M008}}zones d'emploi{{M009}}) with monthly observations from October 2018 through July 2024.
