The French {{M001}}diagnostic de performance {{M002}}nerg{{M003}}tique{{M004}} (DPE) is a standardised energy audit of
a dwelling, carried out by an accredited third-party assessor and recorded in a national registry
maintained by the {{M005}}Agence de la Transition {{M006}}cologique{{M007}} (ADEME). It assigns a letter grade
from A to G summarising modelled energy consumption per square metre. Two features make it
unusual as a disclosure instrument. It is certified rather than self-reported, so a displayed
grade can be checked against the registry. And the registry is centralised and public, so the
{{M008}}existence{{M009}} of an audit is verifiable independently of both the seller and the listing
platform. The second property makes the empirical exercise in this paper possible.
