The empirical analysis draws on two sources matched at the listing level, together with transaction records used separately. Listing records are sourced from SeLoger, a major French residential real-estate platform covering sales across metropolitan France. For each listing, we observe the listing price, property characteristics ({{M001}}: area, number of rooms, floor, construction era, location), the disclosure indicator {{M002}}, and, when {{M003}}, the displayed EPC letter (A through G). Transaction records are not matched to individual listings; we use them to estimate the mapping from listing price to realized sale price ({{M004}} and {{M005}} in equation{{M008}}{{M009}}), which supports treating the listing price as the relevant outcome for the disclosure decision. The ADEME registry is the official national database of certified Diagnostic de Performance Énergétique (DPE) audits, maintained by France's environment and energy management agency and reported by accredited third-party assessors. We match listings to the ADEME registry using address, property type, area, and floor, assigning to each listing an indicator {{M006}} if a certified audit record exists at the time of listing, and {{M007}} otherwise. For the structural estimation, listings for which no address is available are dropped, as the ADEME match requires a geocoded address; these account for a small share of the total sample.
