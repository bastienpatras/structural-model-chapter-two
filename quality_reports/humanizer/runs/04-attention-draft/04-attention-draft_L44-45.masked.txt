Three features of the French setting make this behaviour plausible for apartment buyers in particular. First, a listing that discloses shows the rating as a coloured label and, from January 2022, an estimate of the annual energy bill in euros, so disclosure itself puts energy into the buyer's comparison of dwellings.{{FN1}} A listing that shows nothing does not. Second, heating in many apartment buildings is collective and paid through service charges, so apartment buyers are likely to see energy as part of a monthly charge rather than as an attribute of the dwelling. Third, a missing rating is common: about {{M001}} percent of silent apartment listings match no post-reform certificate in the registry. Silence is therefore unremarkable rather than conspicuous.
