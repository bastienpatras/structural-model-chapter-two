The gross value adds back {{M001}} because, in the bonus form, {{M002}} is not the value of a dwelling without an energy bill. It is the value of an inefficient dwelling, whose energy cost is already netted out of the intercept. Adding {{M003}} moves the zero point of the valuation from the worst dwelling to a dwelling with no energy cost, so that every dwelling, inefficient ones included, carries its energy cost explicitly. The level of {{M004}} never matters for a buyer who treats every listing alike, because it cancels from every price gap; what the cost form adds is the ability to compare what a buyer subtracts on one listing with what she subtracts on another.
