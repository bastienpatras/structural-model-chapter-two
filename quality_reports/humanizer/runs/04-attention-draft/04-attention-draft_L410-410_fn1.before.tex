At the apartment estimates an error of 0.1 in $\hat e^*$ moves $\hat\psi_\delta$ by about $0.1\times\attSensEApt$. In house markets the sensitivity is about \attSensRatio{} times larger, as in Section~\ref{sec:equilibrium}, and the measurement of the silent pool matters correspondingly more there. For the same reason, the restriction $\sigma_1=0$ imposed in $\hat e^*$ (Section~\ref{sec:beliefs}) matters little for the apartment estimate.
