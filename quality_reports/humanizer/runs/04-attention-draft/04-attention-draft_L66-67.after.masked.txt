Section{{M005}}{{M006}} writes the valuation of an EPC-sensitive buyer in what we call {{M007}}bonus form{{M008}}: she adds {{M001}} times her belief that the dwelling is efficient. This form records the difference in energy value between efficient and inefficient dwellings, {{M002}}, but never the level of the energy cost of either, and it has no place for a buyer who forms no belief at all, because every sensitive buyer enters through some number {{M003}}. The ceiling documented in Section{{M009}}{{M010}} is thus a property of the representation as much as of the economics. To ask what a buyer pays for a listing whose energy performance she does not consider, we rewrite the valuation in {{M011}}cost form{{M012}}. The rewriting changes nothing in the model of Section{{M013}}{{M014}} (Lemma{{M015}}{{M016}}); it changes what the model can express. Appendix{{M017}}{{M018}} explains why the normalisation implicit in the bonus form becomes binding once attention depends on what a listing shows.
