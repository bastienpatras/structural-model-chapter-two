The effective belief is a weighted average of the belief of buyers who think about silence and the default of those who do not, with weight {{M001}} on the belief. The two pieces live on different ranges. The Bayesian belief is a probability, so {{M002}}. The default-implied belief is not: it exceeds one whenever {{M003}}, that is, whenever a buyer who ignores energy implicitly assumes a lower cost than that of an average efficient dwelling. A buyer who prices no energy cost at all treats a silent listing as better than a verified efficient one, because even a verified efficient listing shows a positive cost.
