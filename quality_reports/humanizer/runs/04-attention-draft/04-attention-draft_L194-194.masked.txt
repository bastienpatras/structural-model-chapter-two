In Section{{M002}}{{M003}} the hiding rate is pinned down by a feedback loop: more concealment worsens the pool, the worse pool lowers the belief, and the lower belief lowers the gain from further concealment. Lemma{{M004}}{{M005}} says that under limited attention this loop runs at a fraction {{M001}} of its full strength. Inattentive buyers keep subtracting the default whatever the pool contains.
