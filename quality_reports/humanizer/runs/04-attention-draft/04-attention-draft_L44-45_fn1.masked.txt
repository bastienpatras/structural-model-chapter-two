Since 1 January 2022, advertisements for the sale of a dwelling must state the estimated annual energy expenditure for standard use, with the reference year of energy prices, and dwellings rated F or G must carry the mention ``logement {{M002}} consommation {{M003}}nerg{{M004}}tique excessive''. Omitting these mentions exposes the advertiser to fines under Article L126-33 of the Code de la construction et de l'habitation. {{M001}}
The obligation also gives a dated test of the mechanism: under limited attention the gap between silent and rated listings should widen again at that date.
