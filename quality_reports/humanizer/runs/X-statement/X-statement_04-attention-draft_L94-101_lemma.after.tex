\label{lem:cost_form}
The cost-form valuation~\eqref{eq:cost_valuation} reproduces the valuations of Section~\ref{ss:model_setup} exactly, with $\beta_1=C_0-C_1$. An EPC-sensitive buyer subtracts $\mathcal C=C_\theta$ for a disclosed rating of quality $\theta$ and $\mathcal C=\bar C^{\rm sil}$ for a silent listing, where
\begin{equation}
    \bar C^{\rm sil}\;\equiv\;\mathbb E[C\mid d=0]\;=\;e\,C_1+(1-e)\,C_0
    \label{eq:csil}
\end{equation}
is the expected energy cost of the silent pool under the belief $e$ of~\eqref{eq:belief_general}. An EPC-insensitive buyer subtracts $\mathcal C=C^{\rm ins}\equiv\bar V(X)-v_0(X)$, the same amount for every listing with attributes $X$. $C_0$ is the only constant which, added to $\beta_0+\sum_n\beta_nX_n$, makes the two forms agree for both qualities.
