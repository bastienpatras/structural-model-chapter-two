\label{prop:scale_nonid}
Under the benchmark pricing rule $\alpha_k=\beta_k$ (Section~\ref{sec:model}), fix any
parameter vector with $\rho_m\in(0,1]$ and $\beta_1>0$ for all markets $m$, and any
$\lambda\in(0,(\max_m\rho_m)^{-1}]$. The reparametrization $(\rho_m,\beta_1)\mapsto
(\lambda\rho_m,\beta_1/\lambda)$ is admissible and induces an identical joint distribution of
observed prices, an identical equilibrium hiding rate $\sigma_0^*$, and an identical
equilibrium regime, in every market and every period. Consequently $\{\rho_m\}$ and $\beta_1$
are not separately identified by any price-based moment implied by the model, for any number of
markets $M$; only the composites $\{b_m\}$ and $\{c_{0,m}\}$ are.
