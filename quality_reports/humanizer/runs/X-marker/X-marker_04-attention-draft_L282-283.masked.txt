The three regimes keep their logic, and the central result of Section{{M002}}{{M003}} survives intact: in Partial Pooling the non-disclosure premium equals the hiding cost, because the marginal hider is indifferent. That statement concerns the marginal seller, not buyers, so it holds whatever buyers attend to. What limited attention changes is where silence sits among the ratings, and with it which seller gains most from silence. The next proposition derives the price of each type of listing and the payoff of each type of seller. Uninformed sellers cannot hide a rating they do not have, so they bear no hiding cost.
